L’Épicerie du Siam was born from the association of two French people, expatriates in Thailand for a long time. Both are passionate about gastronomy, and the marriage of tasty products that can be found on the markets of Thailand with our ancestral recipes. 
They have decided to pool their skills to offer you a range of original and traditional specialties, without colorings, artificial flavors or preservative additives.
