Health insurance in Thailand

Why take out health insurance in Thailand?

Living in Thailand means sunshine, good food and excellent hospitals — but many expats only realise later that private medical bills can be steep, with payment or a guarantee often required before treatment.

Excellent care… at a price

International hospitals in Thailand (Bangkok Hospital, Bumrungrad, Samitivej, etc.) match Western standards. Still, a specialist visit can run €100–200, and one hospital stay can reach several thousand euros. Without insurance, you pay upfront.

Cover that fits expat life

Whether you are retired, on a long-stay visa, a digital nomad or here for work, international health insurance typically gives you:

  • private hospital treatment in Thailand
  • freedom to choose your doctor or clinic
  • continuity when travelling in Asia
